title: A guard that fails to evaluate falls through to the next entry instead of stopping the decision
finding: 'internal/realtime/session.go:513: the decider records a guard evaluation error (other than ErrNilOperand) and continues to the next entry of on.<type>. Before this change a failed guard meant no move. With lists, an error on a guarded entry can route the post to a later entry, which may be an unguarded catch-all with outputs (run_as or grants). If a poster can craft a message that makes the guard error rather than return false (for example a host function that fails on a malformed string, or a step-budget overrun), they skip an entry that would have held and reach the fallback. The poster rule and the outputs gate still apply to the fallback, so the poster gets nothing their roles do not allow. Unverified: I did not find a message-controlled eval error, because property types are checked at compile and a nil operand reads as false. The plan (item 5) chose this behaviour on purpose. Fix: stop at the first entry whose guard errors and move nothing, which matches the old behaviour. Alternatively, refuse to fall through to an entry with outputs after an error, and keep the warning.'
severity: minor
resolution: 'Fail closed: a guard that fails to evaluate stops the decision and moves nothing, the warning now says "the session did not move", the guide documents this and why, and TestSessionBranches_GuardErrorStops replaces the fall-through test.'
status: addressed
