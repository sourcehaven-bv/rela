title: NewWorldScope copies the map but not the Chain slices — one tenant can re-scope another's world
finding: 'store/world.go NewWorldScope used maps.Copy, a SHALLOW copy: TypeResolution.Chain is a []entity.Face whose slice header points at the caller''s backing array. The godoc three lines above promised the opposite — ''The map is copied, so the caller may reuse or mutate its input: a WorldScope is handed to every backend and must not change underfoot.'' That promise was false, and For() additionally handed out the shared slice. Verified empirically through the real compiled path (not a synthetic one): mutating chain[0] after construction, and mutating a For() result, both re-scoped the live WorldScope. This matters more than a normal aliasing bug because SharedBase caches ONE worlds.Compiled and hands it to every tenant, so a PR-B/C/D backend doing the natural thing (sorting, filtering or truncating a chain in place) would silently re-scope EVERY tenant''s published world. The failure direction is unbounded: it can turn `select: published` into `select: draft` — the exact leak the feature exists to prevent, reached by a plausible one-line consumer bug. The existing TestNewWorldScope_CopiesInput only mutated the MAP (src["page"] = ...), never src["page"].Chain[0], so it passed against a shallow copy and pinned half of what its name claimed.'
severity: significant
resolution: 'Fixed in 322aa4b6. NewWorldScope now deep-copies via slices.Clone per TypeResolution, and For() returns an owned copy of the Chain so a caller may sort/filter/truncate in place without re-scoping the shared scope. Both godocs now state the guarantee accurately, and NewWorldScope''s explains WHY the copy must be deep (one Compiled per tenant; the unbounded published->draft direction). Pinned by the new TestNewWorldScope_CopiesChains with two subtests — mutating the caller''s chain, and mutating a For() result. Mutation-tested: reverting NewWorldScope to a shallow copy fails subtest 1 (''the scope must not share the caller''s backing array'', published->draft), and dropping For''s clone fails subtest 2 (''For must return a copy the caller owns''). Found by code review, not design review.'
status: addressed
