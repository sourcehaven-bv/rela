type: review-response
title: sqlite triggers created before the column can break a downgrade
finding: schemaSQL installed the content_hash triggers before the v14 rung added the column; after a failed rung a v13 binary would fail every update.
severity: minor
resolution: The triggers are no longer in schemaSQL; init creates them after migrate, when the column exists on fresh and upgraded databases alike.
status: addressed
