Fix: on a move, keep the restart anchors of since: session timers whose name also exists, with since: session, in the target state. Do this in sessionDue/the step''s Due (realtime knows the config), not in ApplySession. Alternatively, make the anchor session-scoped for since: session timers. Add a TestTimers_ClosedFreezes row: reopen, then move contained->assessed, then assert the session is not overdue before T0+172h.'
