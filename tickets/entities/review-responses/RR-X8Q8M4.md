title: The list of refused rela identities is incomplete and lives in the wrong package
finding: "refusedRunAs (internal/cli/identity.go:73-78) and webhookUserPrefix (identity.go:82, a copy of the unexported principal.webhookUserPrefix) refuse realtime, scheduler, automation, provisioner and webhook names. rela also writes as system:mail (internal/mail/script.go:155, the send-script principal shown in delivery audits), system:fs-import (internal/fsimport) and system:gc-sweep (internal/datamigration/gc.go:19). The next rela-owned identity will be missed in the same way, because the list sits in cli and not next to the constants.\t`--run-as system:mail` or `--run-as system:gc-sweep` is accepted, and the audit then cannot tell an operator's write from rela's own mail delivery or GC sweep. The stated rationale (\"must not borrow them\") covers these names.\tMove the registry into internal/principal as one exported predicate, for example principal.IsRelaIdentity(name), next to the constants. Have mail, fsimport and datamigration declare their identities there, and have the CLI call it. Better still, invert the rule: accept only a dedicated namespace such as `system:job:` plus `integration:`, so a new rela-internal name is refused by default."
severity: significant
resolution: 'principal now declares UserMail, UserFSImport, UserGCSweep, UserPerfSeed and SubscriptionUserPrefix, and exports IsRelaIdentity over one registry (exact names plus the webhook and subscription prefixes). mail, fsimport, datamigration, dev seed and the scheduler use the constants; the CLI copy is gone. Did not invert to a system:job: namespace, because existing system:ci-style names are documented and used.'
