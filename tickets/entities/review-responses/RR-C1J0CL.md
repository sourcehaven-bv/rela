title: Every typing refresh wakes all readers three times over; a no-mark refresh is cheaper and still correct
finding: 'Plan D6/step 7 marks c.key on every refresh, and Risks says the cost is "one store Head read plus one Get". In the code it costs more. Local.mark (internal/realtime/local.go:150-178) adds the key to every subscription. The live hub then replays the head topic and the anchor''s channel-list topic for every subscribed reader (channelKeyTopics, internal/dataentry/live_topics.go:51-56) through the full ACL middleware, with only liveParallel=4 slots and liveRoundCap=256 per round shared with all entity updates (internal/dataentry/live.go:45-49). Because expires_at and at change on each refresh, the head hash moves and every reader gets a frame. The SPA''s follow() then issues GET messages?after_rev plus GET head (frontend/src/composables/useChannel.ts:285-312). That is 1 server replay, 1 list replay and 2 client requests per reader, per typist, every 3 s. With 50 readers and 5 typists this saturates the 4 replay slots and pushes real entity frames into the overflow fallback. The rejection of a silent refresh ("readers hide a signal at its old expires_at") applies only because the client expires typing on its own clock. Plan change: (a) TypingView carries {author, since} with since = the start time carried over from the replaced signal, and no expires_at/at that moves on refresh. (b) Typing() marks c.key only when the identity had no live typing signal in that ring (start). A log post that drops it marks (already does), and so does expiry, through the existing scheduleExpiry/sweep marks (local.go:191-219). (c) The SPA does not run useExpiryClock on typing_now; it shows exactly what the last head says, and the server''s expiry mark removes it. Add a live test in internal/dataentry/live_channels_test.go: three refreshes within the TTL produce one frame at start and one at expiry, not one per refresh. Update Risks and the rejected alternative accordingly.'
severity: significant
