## Recommended resolution

AC6 docs and the three-tier contract must make it unmistakable that `custom.js`
is **fully-trusted, unconfined** code while `apps/` is **sandboxed and
confined** — so no reader reasons "apps are sandboxed, therefore custom.js is
too," and no operator pastes untrusted third-party JS into `custom.js` on the
assumption that rela confines it.
