title: NopRedactor on scheduler/cascade weakens the headline guarantee more than the godoc admits
finding: 'ScheduledLuaWriteDeps passes nil → NopRedactor → row gating with NO field redaction. The godoc calls this ''the weaker half of the guarantee but never the wrong one''. That overstates it: the motivating use case is ACL-bound LLM jobs, and the AC1 demonstration of value (salary redacted from a readable person) is exactly what does NOT hold on the scheduler path — a scheduled job reading person gets salary in full. Row gating bounds WHICH entities, nothing about fields, and field-level visible: is the finer-grained half operators will assume applies (especially since run_as exists to narrow a job). Either wire an affordance resolver into appbuild, or state the limitation in user-facing scheduler docs next to run_as, not only in a Go godoc.'
severity: significant
resolution: 'Overstatement removed. The godoc now reads KNOWN LIMITATION and states plainly that field-level visible: redaction does NOT apply on the scheduler path — a job that may read a type receives all its properties — and that closing it means wiring an affordance resolver into appbuild. Surfaced for operators in docs/scheduled-tasks.md next to run_as, not only in Go godoc.'
status: addressed
