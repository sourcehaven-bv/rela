title: Busy wake is not narrowed to channels the subscriber can read, so a stale board frames at the moment of an unreadable move
finding: 'internal/dataentry/live_busy.go:63-74 (recordBusy) puts every row of the page into the wake. internal/realtime/busy.go:78 reports ok=true even when the principal lacks the channel type''s read role on some or all of those rows; that role can be local, so the check is per anchor. A session move on such an unreadable channel on a visible card selects the board (selectsBusy). Normally the replay hashes the same and sends nothing, as live_busy.go:27-29 says. That claim fails when the board''s hash is already stale from a move in another process that the safety replay has not caught yet (up to about 14 s, live_inbox.go liveInboxSafetyMax). In that case the replay frames at the tick of the unreadable move. That tells the subscriber when a channel it may not read moved. Preconditions: a multi-process deployment and a cross-process move within the safety window, so this is a one-bit timing channel on a visible entity, not entity existence. Fix: have BusyReader.Busy return the anchors whose channels the caller may read under the type''s role (busyRun.prime already decides this), and record only those in the wake. Otherwise, correct the live_busy.go comment to name this residual.'
severity: minor
reason: '(comment corrected): the read role can come from the channel creator (roleAdmits SourceCreator), so readability is known only for channels that exist and are busy now, not for idle or not-yet-created ones. Narrowing per anchor would add an anchor read and a role prime for every row of every busy list request and would still leave the creator case open. The live_busy.go package comment now names the residual: when the board hash is stale from a cross-process move inside one safety period, a move on an unreadable channel of a visible card can time that frame to within one tick. This is one bit of timing, never entity existence.'
status: wont-fix
