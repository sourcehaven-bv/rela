title: Queued posts are never checked again when their revert event is lost, so an overflow or reconcile posts stale enter, leave or change messages, even for deleted entities
finding: 'internal/realtime/trigger.go effects/enqueue and round.compare. A queued post is worked out against the seen-set, which stays as it was until the post is made. Only a later Note of the same id replaces or dequeues it. When that event is lost, the safety nets cannot repair the queue. On pending overflow (addPending drops a type''s ids and sets full) and on a reconcile, round.compare returns only ids whose current state differs from the seen-set. An entity that went back to its seen state, or a never-seen entity that was queued for enter and then deleted, does not differ, so it is not a candidate and its queued post stays. postOne then posts it without reading the entity again, and sh.apply records the stale fp or match. For an On=enter rule, a queued enter for an entity that was then deleted posts into a channel for a missing entity, and a cardinality-one channel is created for it. For an On=change rule, a revert to the seen fp posts the stale change, records fp2, and the next reconcile posts a second change back. Overflow happens under exactly the load that fills the queues (64 posts per 500 ms against a queue of up to 65536, which is about 8.5 minutes of backlog), so this is a regression introduced by the queue. Fix: in compare (and for a full type in a round), add the ids of every queued ref of the type''s active rules to out, so they are read and worked out again. Alternatively, have postOne read the row again before it posts. Add a test: queue an enter past the cap, delete the entity while pending is in overflow, run a round and a reconcile, and assert nothing is posted.'
severity: significant
resolution: A full compare (overflow or reconcile) now adds every id with a queued post to the candidates, and working out an id also visits its queued refs, so a deleted or reverted entity drops its waiting post. TestTriggers_StaleQueuedPostIsDropped covers delete under overflow, delete with reconcile and revert with reconcile.
status: addressed
