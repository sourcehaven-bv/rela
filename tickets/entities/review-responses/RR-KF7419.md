title: 'Cosmetic: undefined CSS variable, stray blank lines, misleading scoping comment'
finding: |-
    Three small things.

    (1) `SettingsView.vue` used `var(--action-pair-min-width, 7rem)` for a custom property declared nowhere in the codebase, so the fallback always applied — a var() whose property does not exist is a lie about configurability.

    (2) All ten @keyframes deletions left a double blank line behind, making the next diff noisier than it needs to be.

    (3) The `.form-actions > .btn` comment reads as though not reaching DynamicForm's footer is intentional design, when it is a side effect of the style being `scoped`. Within SettingsView any future action row silently inherits the 7rem min-width.
severity: nit
resolution: |-
    (1) Replaced with the literal `7rem` plus a note explaining why the indirection was removed — the alternative (declaring the property in scales.css) would add a token nobody asked to configure.

    (2) Collapsed across all ten files.

    (3) Left as-is deliberately. The comment's substantive claim — that equal-width grouping is for genuine action PAIRS and must not reach DynamicForm's five-control footer — is correct and is the thing worth preserving; whether the mechanism is scoping or selector choice does not change what a future editor needs to know. Rewriting it risks obscuring the actual constraint.
status: addressed
