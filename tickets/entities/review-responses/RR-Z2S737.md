Scope was kept narrow on purpose. The re-render mechanics this component shares
with DocumentView (in-flight mounting, the identical-HTML guard, the generation
fence) are pinned once in `DocumentView.rerender.test.ts` and are not
re-asserted here; duplicating them would double the maintenance for no added
signal. What the new file covers is the one thing that is genuinely per-file:
whether this component acts on the classifier's answer.
