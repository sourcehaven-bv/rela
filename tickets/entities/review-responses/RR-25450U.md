id: RR-25450U
type: review-response
title: kvstate rewrites the whole document twice per for_each subject
finding: ExtendLease (always forward) and SettleChild each rewrite and fsync the whole document, which holds every run's Subjects map. At the 10,000-subject cap that is ~20,000 writes of several hundred KB, serialized behind the mutex that the tick's Load and CreateRun also take.
severity: significant
resolution: kvstate keeps each for_each subject in its own small KV key (hex-encoded run and subject ids) plus one index key per run written once by ExpectChildren. The document holds only counters, so a settle writes one tiny key and the small document. StartChild skips lease writes under one minute (leaseStep). Subject keys of pruned or trimmed runs are deleted after the document write.
status: addressed
