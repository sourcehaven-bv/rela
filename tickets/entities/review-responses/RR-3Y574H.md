title: Adapter emits empty strings for unpermissioned surfaces; shadowed perms variable in checkDeadPermissions
finding: Two small code-quality issues. (1) internal/cli/acl.go:239-257 appends Permission for every surface including the empty ones, so a real data-entry.yaml with 100 nav entries and 2 permissions emits 98 empty strings, filtered downstream at tier_a.go:207. The consumer-side filter is correct defensive practice for a public interface and should stay, but the adapter should not emit known junk at the source; the per-surface test failure output even prints `got [""]`, which is noise where signal is wanted. (2) internal/aclaudit/tier_a.go:190 names the PermissionConsumer parameter `perms`, and :212 declares a local `perms := append(...)` that shadows it inside the loop. It is correct — the consumer is fully drained before the shadow — but a reader tracking `perms` has to verify that rather than see it.
severity: nit
status: open
---

## Fix

1. Skip empty permissions at the source (`if e.Permission != ""`) in each
adapter loop; keep the consumer-side filter as belt-and-braces.
2. Rename the local in `checkDeadPermissions` to `rolePerms`.

Also worth a comment on `navigationPermissions`: it recurses even though
`NavigationEntry`'s own doc (`dataentryconfig/config.go:620`) says nested groups
are not supported. Over-collecting a permission is harmless for an audit;
under-collecting is the bug. Saying so stops the next reader thinking the
recursion contradicts the schema.
