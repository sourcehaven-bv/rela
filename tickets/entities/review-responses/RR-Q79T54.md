title: Delete-capture is placed AFTER the committed delete → non-atomic, non-self-healing loss
finding: 'The plan pins synchronous delete-capture at manager.go:618, but that is AFTER the store delete already committed at manager.go:598 (Store.DeleteEntity → pgstore hard-deletes the row in its own tx, entity.go:324/345). A crash between :598 and :618 loses the final pre-delete version PERMANENTLY — and unlike the sweep''s accepted best-effort gap, this is NOT self-healing (the row is gone; no future sweep can recover it). This breaks the marketed guarantee ''delete is synchronous so nothing lost on delete'' (R3) and AC-2. The in-memory `current` entity is available at :563, so the content exists. Fix: capture the version BEFORE the Store.DeleteEntity call at manager.go:598 (order-before). Full atomicity (capture inside the store''s delete tx next to writeTombstonesForEvents, entity.go:339) is the stronger option but reverses ''store never learns Principal'' — defer that to TKT-N0OWKE. Order-before is the minimal v1 fix and closes all but a vanishingly small window.'
severity: significant
resolution: 'Fixed: the final pre-delete version is captured BEFORE the Store.DeleteEntity call (before manager.go:598, using the in-memory `current` entity loaded at :563), not after at :618. This closes the permanent non-self-healing loss window. Full intra-tx atomicity remains the stronger option, deferred to TKT-N0OWKE. AC-2 asserts the final pre-delete version exists after delete.'
status: addressed
