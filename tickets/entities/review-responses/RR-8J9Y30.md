## Finding

Two defects in `internal/analysis/states.go`, both consequences of removing the
headless-state invariant:

1. The `headless-family` finding flagged any entity holding a named face with
no zero-coordinate row. That is now the normal and correct shape, so `rela
analyze` reported correct graphs as corrupt. An operator gates a merge on this
command, so a false positive here is not cosmetic.
2. The type-mismatch check was gated on `hasDefault`, meaning it skipped
faced families altogether — the check silently did nothing precisely where it
was most needed.

## Resolution

Removed the `headless-family` finding entirely.

Reshaped `stateFamily` to `{famType string; states []stateRow}`, taking
`famType` from the first row seen rather than from the zero coordinate, and
removed the `hasDefault` gate so type mismatches are detected on faced families.
