title: Pending batch untombstoned onto a re-created entity reusing the id
finding: internal/realtime/retention.go:231-236 restores a pending batch whenever the anchor exists after OrphanGrace. It cannot tell a rolled-back delete from a committed delete whose confirm was lost (crash, DB error, hook timeout, or a non-leader whose wake at internal/appbuild/realtime_worker.go:127 is process-local) followed by a re-create of the id. The old entity's channels and audit then attach to the new entity and are readable by its viewers, and a delete-policy batch is resurrected instead of erased.
severity: significant
resolution: EntityCreated confirms the id's batches, and the store refuses to untombstone a final batch.
status: addressed
