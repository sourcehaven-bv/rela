title: Show-all toggle and config reload shift the reader's view
finding: 'Scroll anchoring runs only in the watch on props.messages (RlChannel.vue:261-282). Turning the show-all toggle on or off, a config reload that adds or removes display, and a reveal all change the rendered rows without changing messages. A reader who is scrolled up and presses the toggle has every hidden or collapsed message inserted above the viewport, and the view jumps. The plan''s risk section says the anchor logic works unchanged, which holds only for message changes. Change the plan: take the anchor before any change to rows and restore it after (watch rows, or the inputs showAll, expanded and presentation, with flush pre), and add a story or test where the toggle is pressed while scrolled up.'
severity: significant
resolution: One keepView helper now captures and restores the anchor for message changes and, through a flush pre watch on showAll, expanded, revealed and presentation, for row changes, with rowFor resolving a seq that moved into or out of a fold (step 6, AC 7). Added the story "Quiet types, scrolled up" that presses the toggle while scrolled up.
