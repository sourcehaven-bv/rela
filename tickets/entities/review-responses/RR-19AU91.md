## Finding

The plan proposed a custom `UnmarshalYAML` for the `create:` bool-or-mapping
union as though it were novel. `DarkMode`
(`internal/dataentryconfig/palette.go:61-95`) already implements exactly this
union **in the same package** — "the two-way union: bool false or an object" —
down to a `Disabled bool` plus `Explicit *T` pointer and `IsDisabled()` /
`IsExplicit()` accessors.

Two lessons the plan got wrong by not looking:

1. `DarkMode` **rejects `true`** with a message telling the operator to write either
`false` or an object. The plan accepted `true` as a synonym for `{}`.
2. `DarkMode` also implements `MarshalYAML`, `MarshalJSON` and `UnmarshalJSON`.
`ViewSection` is tagged for **both** yaml and json on every field and
`ViewConfig` is served over the wire, so `SectionCreate` needs the JSON side too
— four methods, not one.

## Resolution

Plan now names `DarkMode` as the model to copy and adopts its rejection of
`true`: two spellings for one meaning is a thing to explain forever, and the
precedent already chose. `create: true` is a load error, listed under Negative
Tests.

Also reconsidered the simpler `enabled: true`-by-default alternative and
rejected it on inspection: Go's zero value for `bool` is `false`, so a
defaulting-true field needs either a `*bool` or its own unmarshal regardless.
Given the precedent exists, the union is both cheaper and more consistent.
