type: review-response
title: 'Upstream goroutine leak: neoq never stops its cron goroutine on Shutdown'
finding: 'neoq starts a robfig/cron goroutine per backend (memory_backend.go:57) and never calls cron.Stop in Shutdown — it cancels contexts only. Independently reproduced: 20 create/close cycles leak exactly 20 goroutines, all parked in cron.(*Cron).run.'
severity: significant
reason: 'Not fixable from outside the library: neoq exposes no handle to stop the cron. Bounded and harmless for a single long-lived process, which is every current caller — one goroutine for the life of the process. Documented at neoqQueue.Close with the measurement and, importantly, the condition under which it stops being acceptable: appbuild.SharedBase documents a one-Services-per-tenant shape, and evicting tenants would leak a goroutine per eviction. Fix upstream before relying on tenant churn.'
status: wont-fix
