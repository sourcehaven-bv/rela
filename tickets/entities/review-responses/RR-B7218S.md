title: Per-directory granularity does not help relations, the largest and most often changed directory
finding: relations/ is one flat directory (10,413 files in tickets/, 12 ms to list, more than all entity directories together), and nearly every entity create also writes a relation. So after any write session the next open rescans relations anyway, and AC3's saving applies only to the cheaper entity side. The "Alternatives rejected" reasoning (per-directory costs one ReadDir per changed directory) overstates the benefit. Either use this to support dropping the cache, or state in the plan that relations are expected to be rescanned after most writes and that the gain is limited to entity directories.
severity: minor
resolution: Resolved by removing the cache; per-directory granularity is no longer planned.
status: addressed
