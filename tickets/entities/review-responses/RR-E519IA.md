title: Broken reminder props back off whole channels and can starve deadline timers
finding: 'internal/realtime/timers.go:621-630 (Timers.remind) puts the channel into holdBack when reminderProps fails. The failure comes from config (literal props against the metamodel), so a single metamodel change breaks every channel of the type in the reminder''s in: states at once. Two consequences. (1) While held back, the channel''s interrupting after: timer cannot fire either, because fireDue skips backoff keys before it checks fire (timers.go:530). A 72h deadline is then up to TimerBackoff (5m) late per cycle, for as long as the props stay broken. (2) Once more than MaxTimerBackoffKeys (128) channels fail, holdBack evicts the oldest entries. The overdue failing channels have the oldest hints, so they fill the head of the ListSessionsDue page (limit = 65 + len(backoff), so at most 193). Healthy channels, including due deadline timers, are never listed. The tail check at timers.go:561 sets More, so the loop spins at TimerFloor (1s) and logs about 65 errors per round. Fix: reminder props are pure config with no per-channel input (today() is refused at load). Compute them once per (type, reminder) per round or per config pointer. When they fail, log once per config and skip that reminder for every channel: no remind, still fire or reschedule, and fold the timer due time. Do not use the per-channel backoff for this. Optionally write the hint past the broken reminder so it leaves the head of the listing. Add a TestTimers_RemindPostFails row with more than 128 broken channels and one due timer behind them, and assert that the timer fires.'
severity: significant
resolution: 'Reminder props are checked once per reminder per config; a failure logs once and pauses only that reminder; timers still fire and no channel backs off. Tests: TestTimers_RemindPostFails (deadline on time), TestTimers_RemindPostFailsManyChannels.'
status: addressed
