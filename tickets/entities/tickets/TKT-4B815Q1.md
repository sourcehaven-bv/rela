## Description

archplan v26.11.4 reads the `in` field that seqtrace writes since TKT-EOFAO77.
With it, `structure.repeated_orchestration` no longer reports a shared helper or
a middleware as steps that every handler copied. The pinned exporter v0.2610.30
drops the field.

Raise `archplan_version`, `archplan_commit` and `archplan_go_version` in the
`justfile` together, and re-record `.archplan/baseline.json` from a traced run,
because finding ids differ between versions.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The three pins name v26.11.4, its commit and exporter v0.2611.4.
2. `archplan --version` at the pinned commit prints `archplan 26.11.4`.
3. A traced `archplan ci` against the committed baseline reports no new
findings and no worse ratchet metric.
