## Description

A person who gets a waiting session in the inbox cannot act on it there. Inbox
rows show only the entity, the channel and the state. They have no buttons and
do not show the question. To answer, the person opens the entity page, finds the
channel and acts there.

Show the waiting message on the inbox row, with its decision buttons and its
`choices` (one-click choices plus the "other answer" option), so a person can
answer or approve straight from the inbox.

Reuse the decision buttons (TKT-T9G1AE, TKT-TXZZZ2) and choices (TKT-03FQPD) of
the entity page channel. Do not build a second implementation. Affordances and
gates on the inbox row must match the entity page for the same principal.

Motivating case: the agent runner POC (`rela-agent-runner-poc`). An agent asks a
question or a plan approval and waits; the human answers from the inbox.
FINDINGS.md section 5, generic gap 2. Follows TKT-9YRRM5 and TKT-YK30D1.
